# Vaelora — Faction Visual Language v0.3

## 1. Shared rule

Auren and Brasal use the same core gameplay roster in the initial prototype. Art may differentiate culture, construction and equipment, but must not imply unique unit classes, buildings or bonuses that the GDD does not provide.

## 2. Auren League

### Identity sentence

**Connect first, then make every city count.**

### Spatial language

- civic centre is visible;
- roads/plazas create deliberate alignment;
- structures acknowledge one another spatially;
- vertical markers reinforce routes;
- stage growth expands civic structure coherently.

### Architecture

Primary forms:

- pale masonry masses;
- clean hip/gable roofs;
- arches;
- vertical standards;
- bridge/route markers;
- compact civic hall hierarchy.

Secondary detail:

- blue cloth/roof panels;
- gold route/knot accents;
- warm wood doors/frames;
- restrained red warnings/military accent only.

### Unit language

- upright stance;
- cleaner equipment geometry;
- narrow/vertical shield;
- consistent straps and civic marks;
- field gear organized rather than ornamental.

### Stage progression

**Stage 1:** civic anchor + small support cluster.

**Stage 2:** plaza and route organization becomes unmistakable; supporting buildings form a town.

**Stage 3:** stronger civic landmark, more route markers and denser hierarchy without becoming a castle.

### Never

- full gold roofs;
- high-fantasy palace silhouette;
- giant cathedral landmark unrelated to mechanics;
- pristine sterile repetition;
- generic Roman/medieval copy.

## 3. Brasal clans

### Identity sentence

**Make the frontier feed the clan, then decide where the pressure lands.**

### Spatial language

- workshop/longhouse anchors the settlement;
- yard and storage explain work flow;
- structures respond to local space rather than axis;
- visible additions/repairs create history;
- stage growth feels accretive but deliberate.

### Architecture

Primary forms:

- broad timber roofs;
- reinforced stone footing;
- longhouse/workshop masses;
- kiln/forge/storage silhouettes;
- low work-yard structures.

Secondary detail:

- charcoal iron;
- copper repair straps;
- deep moss accents;
- weathered bone as neutral trim only;
- hooked practical standards.

### Unit language

- wider grounded stance;
- broader shield;
- field pack/tools;
- repaired practical metal;
- layered hide/cloth;
- pressure and readiness without monstrous caricature.

### Stage progression

**Stage 1:** work anchor + small yard/support.

**Stage 2:** visible production/storage logic and clustered clan settlement.

**Stage 3:** expanded work systems, more repair layers and stronger clan marker; not a spiked fortress.

### Never

- skull decoration as default;
- horns as universal identity;
- spikes on every silhouette;
- chaotic scrap heap;
- “evil” black/red coding as the sole differentiation;
- primitive-vs-civilized contrast with Auren.

## 4. Comparative grayscale gate

Place Auren Stage 2 and Brasal Stage 2 side by side, remove color and reduce to 50% runtime scale. A new reviewer should identify which is the planned/civic settlement and which is the adaptive/work settlement without labels.

If not, revise silhouette/layout before palette.

## 5. Comparative material gate

Faction materials may differ, but shared world materials remain consistent. A faction cannot make its territory appear to be a different biome through architecture color spill.

## 6. Heraldry scale

Heraldic marks are strongest on:

- city focal standard;
- unit shield/cloth;
- selected UI panel;
- store/marketing identity.

They should not be stamped on every crate, roof and stone.


## Premium building-function cue rule

When Granary, Barracks, Academy and other gameplay buildings are implemented as modular attachments, each must gain one dominant silhouette/function cue before decorative trim is considered. Examples: storage mass/ventilation rhythm for Granary, training-yard/standard language for Barracks, vertical study/light/record motif for Academy. The cue is adapted to Auren/Brasal craft but the gameplay function remains recognizable across both.

Do not solve building readability with labels, palette-only changes or extra ornament. Shape/ground plan/function prop comes first.
