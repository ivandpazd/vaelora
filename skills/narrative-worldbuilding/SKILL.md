## Activation Contract
Load for lore, faction identity, naming, narrative content, world references, or in-game text.

## Stage Scope
Primary: stages 0–1; supports stages 2–5. Canon and terminology must be locked before dependent content production.

## Hard Rules
- Preserve the Trama, Vaelora, Auren League, and Brasal clans as established canon.
- Do not stereotype cultures or use real-world references without a documented rationale.
- Separate narrative flavor from authoritative mechanics and flag any mechanical implication.
- Keep names, terminology, and tone consistent across docs, content data, and UI.

## Decision Gates
- Existing canon: extend it and cite the source.
- New canon: propose it as a documented decision before implementation.
- Player-facing text: check clarity, localization readiness, and accessibility context.

## Execution Steps
1. Read world and civilization sources.
2. Define purpose, audience, tone, and continuity constraints.
3. Create the smallest reusable content set.
4. Check terminology, representation, and mechanics impact.

## Output Contract
Return canon used, new content, continuity notes, player-facing text, and open questions.
